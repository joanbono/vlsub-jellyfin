vlsub-go

Downloads subtitles from opensubtitles.org, matched by file hash. No account or API key is required.

Some uploads store a two-line cue as two cues sharing one timing, which makes the lines render bottom-to-top and misattributes dialogue. When enabled, those cues are merged back together after download.
Rank .srt above other formats. A hash match always wins regardless, since it is the only signal that a subtitle was timed against your file.
